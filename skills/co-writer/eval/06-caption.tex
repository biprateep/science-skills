Comparison of recovered versus true dark matter halo masses for the test set. Left: scatter plot of predicted log halo mass against the true value from the simulation, with the one-to-one relation shown as a dashed line. The tight correlation ($r = 0.94$) highlights the model's excellent predictive performance. Right: residual distribution, revealing a slight negative bias ($-0.03$ dex) at the high-mass end — likely due to the scarcity of massive halos in the training set. Error bars denote the 16th and 84th percentiles.
