Despite these advances, existing simulation-based inference (SBI) methods for weak lensing face three key challenges. First, they require a large number of forward simulations, which are computationally prohibitive at the resolution needed for Stage IV surveys \citep{jeffrey2021, fluri2022}. Second, they are often sensitive to misspecification of the simulator, leading to biased posteriors \citep{cannon2022}. Third, and perhaps most critically, they lack a principled mechanism for incorporating observational systematics such as shear calibration and photometric redshift uncertainties. In this work, we address all three challenges by introducing a hierarchical SBI framework that decouples the cosmological signal from nuisance effects — a paradigm shift in how we approach lensing inference.
