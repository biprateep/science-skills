In this paper, we present a novel deep learning framework for atmospheric retrieval of exoplanet transmission spectra. Leveraging a state-of-the-art variational autoencoder architecture, our approach seamlessly integrates physical priors with data-driven inference — enabling rapid, robust, and interpretable characterization of atmospheric properties. We demonstrate that our method achieves a remarkable 40$\times$ speedup over traditional nested sampling approaches while maintaining comparable posterior accuracy (median absolute error of 0.12 dex in log-abundance). Notably, the model generalizes to out-of-distribution spectra, underscoring its potential for the JWST era. Our results pave the way for population-level atmospheric studies of thousands of exoplanets.
