# Counterexamples — sentences the author rejected

Sentences co-writer produced that the author rejected, each with his objection
verbatim. The digest ([SKILL.md](../SKILL.md), "Maintenance") moves them here
from the session log's `Note` field and from `corrections.md`; nothing is
paraphrased.

**How this file is read.** Once, after a draft is written, as examples of what
missed — never as a list to revise against. A passage revised against a list
gets worse. Where a sentence of yours does what one of these does, re-read the
specimen for that slot in [voice-profile.md](voice-profile.md) and write the
stretch again.

**Entry form.** The sentence as delivered; the slot; the objection, verbatim;
what he accepted instead, if he wrote one. Entries with the same fault are
merged, the objections kept side by side.
